How can I help?

This demo understands a few clear request types using simple rules.

You stay in control.

Tasks and list items can be saved immediately. Purchases, bookings and messages become drafts for review. They are never sent or paid for by this demo.

Appointments you enter are your records; Tidy does not check that a provider has confirmed them.
